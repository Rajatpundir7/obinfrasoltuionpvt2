Our Story

OM BALAJI CONSTRUCTION came into existence in 1999, and since then has successfully serviced over 250 clients in more than 100 locations across India. The company specializes in helping organizations meet their outsource manpower needs, providing a wide range of staffing solutions on a contractual or permanent basis.

We are committed to creating excellence in the manpower outsourcing industry by offering innovative staffing solutions and reliable services. Our focus on innovation, opportunity creation, and excellence has made us a trusted partner for infrastructure projects across the nation.
